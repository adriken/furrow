Installs like any other app. It gets its own icon and opens full screen.

Get it on Google Play

iPhone and iPad

Apple does not offer an install button, but it takes four taps.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Furrow now appears on your home screen.
